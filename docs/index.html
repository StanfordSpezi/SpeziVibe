What is SpeziVibe

Skills your AI coder didn't know it needed

SpeziVibe is a collection of installable skills for AI coding tools. Each one packages real product, clinical, regulatory, and platform guidance into something your AI assistant can actually use — so you spend less time prompt-wrangling and more time building.

From Stanford's Spezi initiative, built to lower the barrier to high-quality digital health experiences.

How it works

Three steps. Zero boilerplate.

01

Install the skills

One command adds every skill to your AI coding tool. Works with Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, and Gemini CLI.

02

Describe your app

Tell your AI what you want to build. SpeziVibe skills automatically guide it through clinical, regulatory, and technical decisions.

03

Ship with confidence

Get structured outputs — data models, compliance plans, UX flows, and implementation milestones — not just raw code.

11 Skills

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Each skill is focused, composable, and grounded in real-world digital health workflows.

☆

build-an-app

Walk through the full process of building a digital health app — describe what you want to build and it figures out which planning skills you need, runs them in order, and hands off to implementation.

// Describe your app, SpeziVibe runs the right skills
import { buildApp } from 'spezivibe';

await buildApp({
  needs: 'biodesign-analysis',
  compliance: 'HIPAA + FDA',
  data: 'FHIR R4',
  ux: 'patient-journey',
});
⚙

spezi-platform-selection

Choose the right app foundation — helps you decide whether a project is better served by the React Native Template App or the Spezi Template Application for Apple Platforms.

♥

biodesign-needs-finding

Turn an idea into a real clinical need — guides a team through a Stanford Biodesign-style needs-finding process so they define the problem well before jumping to a solution.

📚

digital-health-study-planning

Plan a study around the app — helps shape a digital health study or research workflow, including recruitment, consent, assessments, schedules, and outcome measures.

Critical Path

digital-health-compliance-planning

Think through privacy and regulatory risk early — helps teams reason about HIPAA, IRB, FDA, GDPR, and adjacent compliance questions before implementation gets too far ahead.

📊

health-data-model-planning

Shape the app's health data backbone — helps define core health concepts, entities, relationships, lifecycle states, and interoperability needs before implementation begins.

📱

digital-health-ux-planning

Design a patient- and clinician-friendly experience — helps plan onboarding, core journeys, engagement loops, and day-to-day workflows for digital health products.

📈

fhir-data-model-design

Map clinical concepts to FHIR — translates clinical requirements into a FHIR R4-oriented data model with concrete resources, relationships, and implementation guidance.

📋

app-build-planner

Turn planning outputs into a milestone-based build plan — extracts features from your UX, data model, compliance, and study planning work, and sequences everything into milestones you can build and review one at a time.

📄

keep-a-changelog-generator

Draft changelogs people can actually read — turns git history into structured changelog entries using the Keep a Changelog format and clearer user-facing language.

💬

release-notes-generator

Summarize a release clearly — helps generate release notes that explain features, fixes, and migration concerns in a concise way for real users and collaborators.
